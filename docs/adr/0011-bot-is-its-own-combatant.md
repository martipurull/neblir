# A bot is its own combatant

A bot is a non-sentient machine a character holds, with its own initiative, attributes, and item placements. A character, an item, or a vehicle would bring the wrong rules with it, so a bot is its own catalogue domain. The combatant is the bot holding.

## Considered Options

- **GM-controlled character:** the sheet, Owners, Status, and death rolls already exist. A bot is not sentient, and a second Owner would turn the holding character into a player character.
- **Item:** inventory, stacks, and equip already exist. An item is not a combatant and cannot hold other items.
- **Vehicle:** Official, Custom, and Unique, plus a holding, already match the catalogue shape. A vehicle is not a combatant, and its mounted gear is not a separate equip from the character.
- **Own catalogue domain (chosen):** Official bot, Custom bot, and Unique bot, plus a bot holding. The character holds the bot and is not an Owner of it.

## Consequences

- Play control of the holding character operates the holding. The user who minted a Unique bot edits that record. A play-grant grantee does not mint one.
- A Unique bot has at most one holding. Many characters may each hold the same Official or Custom bot, including two holdings on one character.
- Items stay on the character's inventory. One row is bot-installed, bot-cargo, or bot-carried, and bot equip is separate from the character's equip slots.
- The initiative combatant is the holding. The holding is shared across every game that character is linked to. Each game has its own initiative entry.
- The holding's Condition and battery gate its rolls. Bots use the per-round Reaction counter. A defence spends one. A bot action does not.
- Every bot has the same damage profile. It is not authored on the template.
